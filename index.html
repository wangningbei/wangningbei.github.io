Beibei Wang

Professor · Nanjing University

Rendering · Material Appearance · 3D Computer Vision

I am currently a Professor at Nanjing University, Suzhou campus. My research mainly focuses on rendering and material appearance. I am also interested in 3D computer vision (e.g., surface reconstruction and relighting).

The main target is to create a realistic 3D digital world, including creating (reconstructing and generating) 3D assets (geometries and materials) and rendering these assets realistically.
